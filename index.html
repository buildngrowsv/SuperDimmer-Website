See SuperDimmer In Action

Seven powerful features working together. Intelligent dimming, workspace management, and eye comfort—all automated.

Per-Region Brightness Detection

Works inside any window, in any app. SuperDimmer finds regions that are starkly brighter than their surroundings and softens only those areas — so a white PDF, email, or webpage can sit next to a dark IDE without washing out the whole screen.

👻 Auto-Hide Inactive Apps

Apps you haven't touched for 5+ minutes? Hidden automatically (like pressing ⌘H). Keeps your workspace clean. Configure the delay or exclude specific apps.

Super Spaces HUD

Revolutionary floating HUD for managing macOS Spaces. Switch between desktops with keyboard shortcuts (Cmd+1-9), add notes to each Space, and see all your workspaces in a beautiful overview grid. Visual indicators show visit order and recent activity.

Progressive Dimming

Windows progressively dim based on when you last used them, creating a clear visual hierarchy. Current window bright, recently used slightly dimmed, inactive windows fade further. Your workspace naturally guides your attention.

Idle-Aware Timers

Smart timer pause when you step away. Auto-hide, auto-minimize, and progressive dimming all pause during inactivity. Return to find everything exactly as you left it—no unwanted changes while you're away.

How SuperDimmer Actually Works

Not a whole-screen dimmer. It watches every window, finds the bright regions that clash with your workspace, and softens only those areas.

1

Looks inside every window

Preview, Mail, Safari, Pages, Slack, Chrome — wherever you work. SuperDimmer is not tied to one app or document type.

2

Finds disruptive brightness

It identifies regions whose brightness stands out sharply against neighboring content — the white PDF page, the email body, the article pane you can’t restyle.

3

Dims only that region

A precise overlay covers the bright area. Chrome, toolbars, and already-dark UI stay clear so your layout and contrast remain readable.

4

Runs quietly in the menu bar

Install once, grant Screen Recording for analysis, set your threshold, and leave it on. Comfort follows you from app to app.
